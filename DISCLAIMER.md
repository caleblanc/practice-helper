## Streaming services

The app searches streaming catalogues using each service's own public or
developer API, with credentials you supply from your own account. That part is
metadata only.

**It does not include a downloader for any service.** Audio acquisition is an
external command that you configure. Whether a given tool is lawful for you to
use, and whether it breaches the terms of the service in question, is yours to
determine. In particular:

- **Apple Music** — the app ships a preconfigured command for `gamdl`, which
  uses your own subscription cookies. It is not bundled and must be installed
  separately. Downloaded audio remains subject to Apple's Terms of Service.
- **Spotify, TIDAL, Deezer** — no download command is supplied, because no
  sanctioned one exists. These providers search only unless you configure
  something yourself.

The authors do not encourage circumventing DRM or any use that breaches
applicable law, including the DMCA and its equivalents elsewhere.

## Separated stems

Stem separation runs locally via demucs on audio you already have. The
resulting stems are derivative works of the original recording and carry the
same restrictions.
